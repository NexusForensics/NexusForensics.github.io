About Nexus Forensics

Where evidence leads.

Nexus Forensics Inc. builds software for people who have to explain their evidence to someone else: investigators, analysts, and legal teams working from public online sources.

A digital footprint is more than a screenshot. It is the profiles, posts, comments, images, identifiers, and connections a person leaves across platforms. Our work is turning that footprint into an organized, source-linked case record that the investigator controls.

Our Philosophy

The investigator decides. The evidence explains itself.

Nexus is designed around the confidence investigators need when evidence must be reviewed by someone else.

Preserve what is visible

Capture is directed by the investigator and limited to what an authorized user can see on screen. No background scraping.

Investigator controlled

The investigator decides what enters the case, what deserves attention, and what belongs in the final report.

Local by default

Core desktop workflows can keep case data on the investigator's workstation or organization-controlled storage.

Reviewable intelligence

Potential matches include supporting context, thumbnails, links, and confidence information instead of an unexplained result.

Source trail first

Every finding stays tied to its source URL, local file, hash, and timestamp, so a reviewer can follow it back to the original.

Compliance-conscious

Visible, user-directed workflows help keep collection focused on what an authorized investigator sees and selects.

Our Methodology

From the first public view to the final report.

Every Nexus workflow follows the same five steps, so the record stays understandable from collection through review.

01

See it

Review visible public content in real time through the standard platform view.

02

Capture it

Preserve selected screenshots, visible details, source URLs, and local evidence.

03

Review it

Use OCR, keywords, bookmarks, and visual comparison to identify relevant material.

04

Connect it

Compare identifiers, User Index links, media details, and local case signals.

05

Report it

Produce source-linked reports with notes, thumbnails, hashes, and manifests.
