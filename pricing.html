Pricing

Per-seat pricing for Nexus Social Recon.

Nexus Social Recon is licensed per investigator, per year, with platform coverage and analysis capability unlocked by tier. Every tier includes case management, local indexing, SHA-256 hashing, audit logging, and structured evidence reports.

How Licensing Works

Modules unlock directly through license activation.

Platform and capability modules are unlocked per seat when a license is activated — no separate installer or manual configuration step. Every tier ships with the same core case workflow: capture, local index, hashing, audit logging, and reporting.
